R

Rumi Inner Circle — Member Platform

The Challenge

The booking experience was only the first step. Rumi also needed a dedicated digital member experience to strengthen retention, reward loyal customers, and give repeat customers a reason to stay connected with the brand.

What I Built
  • Dedicated member platform with Sign In and Join Now flows
  • Welcome rewards and a points-based member journey
  • Five membership tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Diamond
  • Referral, birthday, and wedding-anniversary rewards
  • Treatment history, therapist ratings, and digital member card
The Strategy

Instead of treating the website as the end of the funnel, the member platform creates a second digital layer — one focused on loyalty, repeat visits, and long-term customer value.

FAQ

Before you book the call

Straight answers to what people usually ask before we talk.

How much does it cost?

Every business has different needs, so I don't post generic pricing here. On the free call, I listen to your situation first, then give a proposal that fits — not a template package.

How soon will I see results?

For quick fixes (landing pages, WhatsApp funnels), results usually show within the first 2-4 weeks. Organic growth and retention systems need 2-3 months of consistency before they stabilize.

Is there a minimum contract?

I prefer to prove results first through a clearly-scoped initial project, then move to a monthly retainer if it's a good fit. No binding annual contracts upfront.

What if the results don't match expectations?

We set realistic targets upfront based on your business's actual situation. If something's off midway, I report it openly and we adjust strategy together — not wait until the contract ends to discuss it.

Do you work alone or with a team?

I personally handle the core strategy and execution, supported by a small team for content production and development when needed. No handoff to an account manager who doesn't know your business details.

Can you work with businesses outside Indonesia?

My current focus is local Indonesian businesses, especially home services, because this playbook is most proven in this market. For outside Indonesia, let's discuss fit on the free call.
